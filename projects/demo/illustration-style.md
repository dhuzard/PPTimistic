# Illustration style — version 1

Palette: restrained teal (#225E63), white, pale gray-green.
Technique: flat geometric vector-like illustration.
Stroke: consistent thin lines; no sketch texture.
Texture: none. Lighting: flat. Perspective: orthographic.
Geometry: simple circles and straight connections. Detail: low.
Background: white or pale gray-green. Proportions: diagrammatic, never empirical.
Complexity: one visual idea, at most five objects.
Negative space: at least one third of the composition.
Composition: centered with safe space for separately editable text.

Master prompt: Create a conceptual scientific communication illustration using the specification above. No text, letters, numbers, charts, or simulated empirical results.
